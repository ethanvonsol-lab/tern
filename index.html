How much does a custom wardrobe cost in Auckland?

It depends on size, layout, finishes and hardware. As a guide, a made-to-measure reach-in typically starts in the low thousands and a fully fitted walk-in sits higher. Use our 30-second estimator for a ballpark, then book a free consult for a fixed-price quote.

Do you offer free measure and quotes?

Yes. We come to your home, measure up, talk through how you use the space and give you a tailored design with a fixed-price written quote. No charge, no obligation, no pushy follow-up.

Should I get a walk-in or a reach-in wardrobe?

A walk-in suits a dedicated room or a large alcove — roughly 1.5 m deep or more. A reach-in makes the most of a standard wardrobe recess. We'll measure your space and show you both options if both fit.

Can you replace just the doors, or just the interior?

Absolutely. We regularly replace tired sliding doors, or keep your existing doors and rebuild the interior with drawers, shelving, shoe storage and double hanging.

How long does it take from quote to install?

Lead times vary with the season and the size of the job. You'll get a firm install date with your quote — and we stick to it.

Where are you based, and what areas do you cover?

Our workshop is at Unit 4/58 Anzac Road in Browns Bay, so the North Shore and Hibiscus Coast are our backyard — and we work right across Auckland, including Central, West, East and South Auckland. Not sure if you're covered? Ask us.

Do you work with builders, architects and new builds?

Yes. We work with homeowners, builders, architects and renovators, and can quote directly from plans for new builds and renovations.

Why not just buy a flat-pack wardrobe?

Flat-pack is built to standard sizes, so it rarely fits a real room — you lose space at the sides and top, and thinner board and hardware wear out faster. A made-to-measure wardrobe uses every centimetre, is installed properly and is built to last for decades.
